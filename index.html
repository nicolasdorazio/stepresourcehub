Run an Event
1
Submit your idea for approval & funding

Alexsys, the Social Committee, and the Directors will review your submission and be in touch.

2
Look into spaces to host this event
  • Wharton buildings (JMHH, SHDH, Dinan, etc.)
  • University Life spaces (ARCH, Houston Hall, etc.)
  • Think beyond classrooms: High Rise Field, Kelly Writers House, Perry World House, Penn Park, College Green, Penn Museum, Arthur Ross Gallery, and more.
3
Pay for it
  • Request a PO at least 3 days before the event, then give the vendor the PO number from Undergrad Finance and this PO instructions document.
  • Get reimbursed through Concur for purchases up to $250, within 5 business days.
    • First time using Concur? Email ug-finance@wharton.upenn.edu with your name and Penn ID.
    • Meals cap at $35 per person for breakfast or lunch and $55 for dinner, tax and tip included.
4
Request a flyer from Marketing

Alicia & the Marketing Committee can help promote your event.

5
Log it afterward

Record the activity, headcount, and a photo.
